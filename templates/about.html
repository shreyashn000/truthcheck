ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology for better information.

TRUTHCHECK is an AI-assisted fake news detection and digital media literacy project developed as part of a Community Engagement Project in B.Sc. Information Technology.

01 — PROJECT OVERVIEW

A practical approach to digital awareness.

The rapid growth of social media, messaging platforms and online news has made information easier to access and share. At the same time, misleading information can spread rapidly.

TRUTHCHECK combines a machine-learning based text classifier with a digital media literacy awareness programme. The system analyses submitted news text and provides a prediction as LIKELY REAL, LIKELY FAKE or UNCERTAIN.

The project also encourages users to verify important claims through original sources and credible evidence rather than relying only on an automated prediction.

05 — IMPORTANT

AI prediction is not fact-checking.

TRUTHCHECK is a machine-learning based classification system. Its result reflects patterns learned from its training data and should not be treated as proof that a news claim is true or false.

For important information, users should check the original source, publication date, supporting evidence and multiple credible sources.
